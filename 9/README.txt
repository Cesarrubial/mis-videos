Fraction Circles by risacher on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:854000

Summary:
Educational tool for teaching fractions.  Circles divided into sectors as halves, thirds, quarters, fifths, sixths, eighths, and tenths, with the fraction written in numbers recessed on one side.  Also includes a frame to fit the sectors into.  STL files and OpenJSCAD source.

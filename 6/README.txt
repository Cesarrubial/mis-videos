Photos currently show previous design and original symbol set - will update when able.Tactile Communication Symbols.  Target audience: individuals who use AAC to communicate and are blind, DeafBlind, or have visual impairments; Pre-Braille learners, individuals with multiple disabilities.  These are abstract communication symbols.  Careful consideration of an AAC learner's communication level is vital to providing appropriate AAC instruction and language modeling.  The Communication Matrix is a great resource for more information.Please feel free to share the STL files with any school/family looking for tactile symbols!  Photos show multicolor prints which provide visual contrast for individuals who are dual or multi-sensory learners.  Symbol bases reflect word types:red/square - placesyellow/oval - peoplegray/scalloped - descriptionsblack/diamond - expressionsblue/rectangle - timewhite/circle - thingsgreen/pentagon - actionsI also sell printed symbols in my shop for those who don't have the time/resources to print themselves or are looking for customization, vocabulary not seen here, and/or concrete communication symbols.
